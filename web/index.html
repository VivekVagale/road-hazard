🛣️ Road Hazard Detection

Finds potholes and road cracks. A YOLO11 detector fine-tuned on 6,000 real Indian road images, running in your browser: images and camera frames never leave your device.

Sample photos are held-out test images the model never trained on, from the RDD2022 dataset (Arya et al., CC BY 4.0), India subset. Cracks are hard: thin and long, so boxes vary a lot between annotators. Model accuracy per class is in the repo.
